The 5 GenUI primitives

What an AI customer-service agent should look like in 2026.

Most agents are a black box: a message goes in, a message comes out, and the human in the loop has no idea why. Bijou's 5 primitives make the agent inspectable, controllable, and continuously improving.

I

Activity Feed

Real-time feed of every AI action on the Home tab. The owner always knows what Bijou just did, even when they're not looking.

GET /api/dashboard/activity
II

Reasoning Trace

Every AI reply is paired with the KB docs it retrieved, the tool calls it made, the model it used, and its confidence. EU AI Act 2024 Article 13, in production.

GET /api/dashboard/messages/{id}/reason
III

Inbox Co-pilot

While the human agent is typing, Bijou surfaces 1–3 reply suggestions. Never auto-sends. Every show + accept + edit + dismiss is audited.

POST /api/inbox-copilot/suggest
IV

A2A Shared Context

One customer, one timeline, every channel. WhatsApp → voice → WhatsApp, interleaved newest-first. The voice AI remembers the WhatsApp chat; the WhatsApp AI remembers the call.

GET /api/shared-context?phone=…
V

AI Activity Stream

Replaces the Updates tab with a live, filterable feed of every AI reply, Co-pilot event, escalation, and channel handoff. Auto-polls every 5s.

The A2A seam

A customer messages on WhatsApp. Five minutes later, they call. The AI remembers both.

This is the actual cross-channel thread from the live demo at /static/cross-channel-demo.html. Mei Lin books a viewing on WhatsApp, follows up on voice, gets confirmation back on WhatsApp. The voice AI sees the WhatsApp context; the WhatsApp AI sees the call.

WhatsApp customer 14:00
Hi, I want to book a viewing for unit 12B this weekend.
WhatsApp Bijou 14:01
Hi Mei Lin! 👋 Unit 12B is available Sat 3pm or Sun 11am — what works?
WhatsApp customer 14:02
Saturday 3pm pls
WhatsApp Bijou 14:03
Booked ✅ I've sent the address and your agent Jason's contact. See you Saturday!
Channel handoff — WhatsApp → Voice
Voice customer 14:06
Hi, it's Mei Lin — I just booked on WhatsApp. Can we move to Sunday instead?
Voice Bijou 14:07
Of course, Mei Lin — I can see your Saturday 3pm booking for unit 12B on WhatsApp. Sunday 11am works.
WhatsApp Bijou 14:08
🔄 Updated: Your viewing for unit 12B is now Sunday 11am (was Sat 3pm). Jason will meet you at the lobby.
Compliance posture

Lawyer-ready. Not legalese.

Three documents in docs/compliance/ cover every framework a Malaysian SME customer will ask about. Written to be readable by outside counsel with no Bijou context.

🇪🇺 EU AI Act 2024

Risk class: Limited risk (Article 50 transparency only — not high-risk under Annex III).
Article 13 traceability: every AI reply has a Reasoning Trace.
Article 14 human oversight: Inbox Co-pilot is a suggestion surface, never auto-sends.

🇲🇾 PDPA 2010 + GDPR + UK GDPR

Right of access / download / erasure: GET /data-request — public, no login.
Affirmative consent (outreach): public.outreach_consent_log — campaign refuses to start without active opt-in.
Soft-revocation: audit trail preserved forever.

🧠 Model Card (Gemini 2.5 Flash)

Lineage: Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash, frozen inference (no fine-tuning).
Intended use: customer-service Q&A over tenant KB; out-of-scope for credit, medical, legal, employment.
Eval suite: 7 categories, quarterly review.

Why now

Three reasons to back Bijou.

1

The customer-service agent market is exploding, and every competitor is a black box.

WATI, SleekFlow, Respond.io, Tidio. None of them show the customer why the AI said what it said. None of them have an A2A seam that survives a channel switch. None of them are EU AI Act 2024 compliant out of the box. Bijou is.

2

The shared nervous system is the moat.

We already have 3 Telnyx voice agents, 1 contact-center, 1 integration gateway — all live, all disconnected. Absorbing them into a single monorepo (3 issues, scoped) makes Bijou the only product in the market that can credibly sell "WhatsApp + voice + integrations" as one product, not three.

3

Self-hostable. EU-grade compliance. Open source.

EU customers and Malaysian enterprises increasingly demand data sovereignty. Bijou runs on the customer's own Coolify instance, with no third-party cloud dependency, and the only third-party model is Google's (DPA'd). We sell to people who can't use SaaS.
