Your data, your rights.

Bijou is built for transparency. If you've ever chatted with a Bijou-powered business on WhatsApp, you can ask for a copy of every message and record we hold — or ask us to delete it entirely.

📋

Get a copy of my data

Download a JSON file with every message, contact, and lead we have for you. Free, usually within minutes.

🗑️

Delete my data

Soft-delete every record. Hard-deletes after 30 days. Reversible for 30 days by replying to the confirmation email.

Request a copy of your data

Use the same number you chat with the business on. We match by digits only.

Delete all my data

⚠️ This is a destructive action. Every message, lead, and contact record for your phone will be soft-deleted immediately and hard-deleted after 30 days.

This page is your right under the Malaysia Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) Sections 12 & 13 and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Articles 15 & 17.

We respond within 21 days (PDPA) or 30 days (GDPR), whichever is sooner. Questions? privacy@mybijou.xyz
